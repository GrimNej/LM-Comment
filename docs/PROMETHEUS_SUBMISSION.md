## Recommended submission positioning

Project name: `LM-Comment`

Tagline:

> Learn to write better replies by seeing, reshaping, and practicing with AI-generated examples.

Short educational statement:

> LM-Comment turns a real communication moment into a short writing exercise. A learner selects text from an Android screen, reviews on-device OCR, compares several AI-generated reply examples, and then edits or rewrites one before copying it. The app never posts automatically, so the learner remains responsible for the final words.
